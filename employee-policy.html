Employee Policy

This Employee Policy sets out the standards and responsibilities that apply to Systematic One employees from the start of employment, including employees on probation.

1. Application of this policy

This policy applies to every Systematic One employee, including employees on probation, from the start of their employment. A separate signature on this policy is not required. Employees are expected to read and follow company policies during their employment. This policy is published on the Systematic One website.

2. Resignation and 45-day notice

Employees must give Systematic One at least 45 calendar days’ written notice before their proposed last working day and work through that period, unless the company agrees otherwise in writing or applicable law requires a different arrangement.

During the notice period, employees must continue their duties and complete a proper handover of projects, client communications, files, credentials, and company property. Verbal notice or an unapproved absence does not replace written notice.

An employee who leaves without working the required notice period will not receive salary for the month they’re leaving on. Systematic One may pursue any further remedy available under applicable law.

Systematic One may shorten or waive the notice period in writing.

3. Confidentiality and protection of company information

Every employee must protect Systematic One’s confidential information and any confidential information entrusted to the company by its clients. This includes source code, designs, client records, project files, proposals, pricing, financial information, credentials, business strategies, internal processes, and employee information.

Employees may access and use this information only for authorized company work. They must not disclose it to an unauthorized person, copy it to a personal account or device without permission, retain it after leaving, or use it for themselves or another business. Employees must promptly report any suspected disclosure, loss, or unauthorized access.

These duties continue after employment ends for as long as the information remains legally protected. A breach may result in disciplinary action during employment. Systematic One may also seek return or deletion of the information, compensation for proven losses, and other legal remedies available under applicable law.

4. Information security

Employees must use approved systems, protect passwords, and access company or client information only for authorized work. They must follow the company’s security instructions and promptly report any suspected data loss, security breach, or unauthorized access.

Employees must not delete, conceal, transfer, or download company or client materials without authorization, including during a notice period.

5. Conflicts of interest and competing work

During employment, employees must not undertake work for a competing business or run a competing venture where it conflicts with their duties to Systematic One. Outside work or business interests that could create a conflict must be disclosed to management.

After employment, former employees must not use or disclose Systematic One’s confidential information, trade secrets, code, client records, or protected materials to benefit a competing business. Any additional restriction on future work applies only to the extent permitted by law.

6. Soliciting or poaching employees

Current and former employees must not misuse confidential information, internal employee records, or their position at Systematic One to deliberately recruit or induce company employees to leave for another business.

For 12 months after leaving, former employees must not directly target Systematic One employees with whom they worked for recruitment through misuse of company information or relationships. This does not restrict general job advertisements or an employee’s independent choice to change jobs. Current employees must not work for another current or former Systematic One employee in a business of the same nature while employed by Systematic One or for 12 months after leaving the company.

Where the evidence establishes unlawful solicitation, misuse of confidential information, or another enforceable breach, Systematic One may take appropriate legal action.

7. Clients and business opportunities

Employees must not divert a Systematic One client, active lead, project, or business opportunity for personal benefit or for another business while employed by the company.

After leaving, former employees must not use confidential client lists, proposals, pricing, project information, or other protected information to divert company business.

8. Ownership of work

To the extent permitted by law and the applicable employment terms, work created as part of assigned duties or using company resources belongs to Systematic One or the relevant client under its agreement with the company. This includes software code, designs, documentation, research, content, processes, and project deliverables.

Employees must save work in approved company systems and cooperate with reasonable steps needed to document or transfer ownership.

9. Company property and accounts

Company devices, documents, access cards, software licenses, accounts, and other property must be used responsibly and returned when requested or when employment ends. Employees must complete a handover and must not keep unauthorized copies of company or client materials.

Systematic One may restrict or disable access when reasonably necessary to protect its systems, clients, or information.

10. Professional conduct

Employees must act honestly and professionally with colleagues, clients, and suppliers. Harassment, discrimination, threats, fraud, bribery, falsifying records, and making unauthorized commitments on behalf of Systematic One are prohibited.

Concerns should be reported to a manager or the company’s designated contact. Reports will be reviewed fairly, and retaliation for raising a concern in good faith is prohibited.

11. Breaches

Systematic One may investigate suspected breaches and take appropriate action, subject to applicable law. This may include disciplinary action, restricting access, requesting the return or deletion of information, or seeking an available legal remedy. The company will follow any legally required process.

12. Updates and applicable law

Systematic One may update this policy and will communicate material changes to employees. If any provision conflicts with applicable law, the law controls. The remaining provisions continue to apply where possible.
